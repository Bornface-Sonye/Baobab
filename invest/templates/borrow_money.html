Borrowing Information

  • The interest rate is automatically retrieved from the latest interest history.
  • Interest is calculated using daily compound interest.
  • The calculated interest amount is used as collateral and is moved from your available balance to your locked balance.
  • The borrowed amount is added to both your available balance and your borrowed balance.
  • Your locked balance cannot be withdrawn until the loan has been repaid.
  • The borrowed funds can be invested immediately, but repayment is required on or before the due date.
